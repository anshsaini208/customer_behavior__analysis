# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

> **An end-to-end data analytics project using Python, PostgreSQL, SQL, and Power BI to understand customer purchasing patterns, product preferences, customer segments, and business opportunities.**

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **3,900 customer purchase transactions** across different product categories to uncover meaningful insights into:

- 👥 Customer demographics
- 💰 Spending and revenue patterns
- 🛒 Product preferences
- 🎯 Customer segmentation
- 🏷️ Discount behavior
- 🚚 Shipping preferences
- ⭐ Product ratings
- 🔔 Subscription behavior

The objective is to transform raw transactional data into **actionable business insights** that can support better marketing, customer retention, product positioning, and subscription strategies.

---

| Tool | Purpose |
|------|---------|
